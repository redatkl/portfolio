GIS & Remote Sensing Specialist

Reda TRANKIL

I am a geospatial analyst and remote sensing specialist based in Morocco, with a deep focus on translating raw Earth observation data into actionable intelligence. My work sits at the intersection of satellite imagery, spatial modeling, and strategic decision-making — helping organizations understand their territory, track change over time, and act with precision.

Over the years I have worked across land use mapping, environmental monitoring, urban expansion analysis, and humanitarian response — always with the same goal: make complex spatial data legible and useful to the people who need it most. I am comfortable working at every stage of the pipeline, from raw sensor data to polished, client-ready maps and reports.

When I am not buried in raster stacks or writing Python scripts for satellite image classification, I am thinking about how geospatial tools can be made more accessible, more open, and more impactful for communities across the Global South.
